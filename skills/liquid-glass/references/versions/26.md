## 2. Motion [M3][M4][M5]

- Press: ×1.34 on a 44 pt button (+15 pt), 100 ms to peak, light blown to white, glyph gone. Grown disc **fuses with neighbours** through a liquid neck while held.
- Release: shape 0.12 s; light fades with the morph ≈ 0.25 s.
- Bar mode change 0.40 s: buttons merge, split, bud off each other. Old glyphs blur 8–10 frames, new sharpen 10 more. Title removed in select mode.
- Banner (27 beta 5 ≈ 26 behaviour): miniature under the clock grows uniformly, τ 150 ms, 0.6 s.
- Switch: 0.42 s. Thin dark line upper-left only, **rainbow fringe** at lower corners. Grey ring round the knob before it dissolves. Melts back over ≈ 4 steps.
- Drag: stretch along velocity up to 10 %.
- App close: icons scale in, wallpaper static.

"26 look" preset: press +15 pt cap 35 %, fuse on press, dispersion 0.06–0.1 on lenses, no hairline, light 315° @ 0.8, shadow (0, 8) blur 40 12 %, frost 7 / 12 / 14.

## 3. System [Apple]

- First Liquid Glass release (WWDC25). HIG Materials: Regular vs Clear, 35 % dimming, no glass in content layer.
- 26.1: Settings → Display & Brightness → Liquid Glass → **Clear | Tinted** (binary). Tinted "increases opacity and adds more contrast".
- `UIDesignRequiresCompatibility` opt-out available.
- Wallpaper zoom on app close removed (returned in 27.3).

## 4. Icons

- One thick glass slab over the whole artwork: milky top-to-bottom fade (translucency ≈ 0.45 + white veil ≈ 0.25), wide soft sheen band (width ≈ 0.02 S, strength 0.35), no dark edge line.
- Shadows between layers wide and soft (blur ≈ 0.045 S, opacity 0.22).
- Overlaps blurred and muddy.
- Highlights follow device tilt.
- Clear style: tile frosted (σ ≈ 4 pt), wallpaper unreadable through it [M2].
- Measured on 40 system icons, 27 / 26: saturation ×1.03, edge sharpness ×1.02, internal contrast ×1.03; blues deeper in 27.
