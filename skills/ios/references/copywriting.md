# Copywriting (Apple voice)

## 1. Principles

- **Short:** titles 1–4 words; descriptions one line; buttons a verb (+ object).
- **Benefit first:** what the person gets, then (optionally) how.
- **Concrete:** numbers, times, places ("14 min", "until 01:00", "−38 % dose").
- **Plain words:** no jargon, no internal names, no source acronyms in descriptions (sources
  go to credits).
- **Confident, friendly, never cute in errors.**
- **Capitalisation:** title-style for buttons and menu items on Apple platforms ("Open in
  Maps"), sentence case for descriptions and footnotes. Pick one rule per product and keep it.

## 3. Buttons and actions

- Primary: verb + result ("Directions · 14 min", "Start the walk", "Open the map").
- Never "OK/Submit" when a specific verb exists. Destructive: name the thing ("Delete Trip").
- Put the useful number on the button when it helps decide (time, price).

## 4. AI copy

- Answer first, reasons second, alternatives last ("Pinta Rooftop fits best: … Also good: …").
- Show work briefly ("Checked 46 places, weather and air quality").
- Suggest 2–3 follow-ups as tappable chips; ask before spending money ("Confirm table").

## 5. Marketing copy

- Hero: one promise in ≤ 8 words, with one emphasised word; sub-head ≤ 25 words.
- Feature titles: 2–4 words; feature body one sentence that a person could say out loud.
- Proof over adjectives: real numbers, real data sources, real product UI.
